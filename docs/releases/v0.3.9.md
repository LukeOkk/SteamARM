**Steam starts more reliably.** Native ARM64 Steam sometimes froze before its sign-in window, most often on the first start of the day: two parts of SteamARM's runtime could wait for each other when Steam's web helper started a helper process. Fixed: 8 of 8 starts afterwards, 15-25 s to the window.

Three faults that sometimes left x86 Steam without a window are fixed too:

- Steam gave up on its web helper at once ("Timed out waiting for webhelper init" in its logs): a signal meant for another process's thread interrupted Steam's short sleeps, so its two-minute wait ended in under a second. On Linux such a signal interrupts nothing, and now it interrupts nothing here either.
- A rare crash inside Apple's code-permission switch (SIGTRAP) when a signal arrived in the middle of it.
- Steam's web helper waited forever for one of its helper processes that had already died; it now notices, as on Linux, and Steam restarts it.

**Copy and paste between the Mac and Android apps.** Text copied on the Mac can be pasted in an Android app, and text copied in an Android app can be pasted on the Mac. Measured in both directions with Spanish and other non-ASCII text.

**Android uses the Mac's keyboard layout.** On a Spanish keyboard, the ñ key types ñ in Android apps (before, Android assumed a US keyboard).

**Disk space:** stopping Steam no longer leaves its 26 MB shared-memory files behind in `/tmp` (a day of starts had left 4.4 GB); **Detener** removes them.

**Runtime:** `readv`, `preadv`, `pwritev`, `preadv2` and `pwritev2` (Linux programs that read or write several buffers at once), and `sigpending` for realtime signals.

**Tested on a real Mac (M4, macOS 27) on the release commit's runtime:** tests/elf 92/92; i386 18/18; Windows D3D9/11/12 probes 11/11; Vulkan device 2/2; ARM64 client checks 4/4; launcher core 35/35; the native display server 4/4 (clipboard included); Android 83 passed with 2 expected failures (arm64 ART's heap; the headless boot's display wall), per-app windows, clipboard both ways, sound, network, clicks and keys included (one earlier run lost Android's zygote right after a quick restart of the session; the rerun passed). Steam from the launcher: x86 main window 8 of 8 (90-95 s); native ARM64 sign-in window 8 of 8 (15-25 s). Records: `benchmarks/stage31-runtime-waits-clipboard.txt`.

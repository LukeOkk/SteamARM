**"Escala de resolución" fixed for Vulkan and Direct3D games.** In 0.3.20 a game drawn at a lower resolution through DXVK or VKD3D reached the screen as one pixel or black: Proton's own full-screen scaler sized its picture from what MoltenVK reports (1x1) and its copy wrote nothing on the Mac. SteamARM now turns that scaler off and enlarges the game itself, over the whole screen, keeping its proportions (black bars where needed).

**Scaling filters: AMD FSR (with sharpness), Apple MetalFX and whole pixels.** **Configuración → Gráficos → Filtro de escalado** decides how a game drawn smaller than the screen ("Escala de resolución") is enlarged: Bilineal (as before), **AMD FidelityFX Super Resolution 1.0** with a **Nitidez FSR** slider, **Apple MetalFX** (Apple's spatial upscaler on the Mac's GPU) or **Píxeles nítidos**. Each app's **Ajustes…** can choose its own. Vulkan and Direct3D games (DXVK, VKD3D).

**NTSYNC (experimental).** **Configuración → Sistema → Sincronización** has NTSYNC: the Linux kernel's NT synchronization driver (/dev/ntsync), emulated by SteamARM across processes, which Proton Experimental and Proton Hotfix use for Windows events, mutexes and semaphores. It passes the Linux kernel's own tests for the driver (12 of 12). Only an explicit choice turns it on; restart Steam after changing it.

**Steam Frame sees your whole disk.** Its root lives in a case-sensitive disk image that was made with 40 GB, so Steam showed 22 GB free. The image now grows to the size of the Mac's disk (it only takes the space of what is in it) and Steam sees the Mac's real free space. The Android image gets the same.

**Steam Frame: no more update-check crashes.** The client checked for SteamOS updates at every start and the check crashed (7 times per start). SteamARM now answers it: "no update available" (the Steam Frame root is updated by SteamARM).

**Privacy.** A home-network address that had slipped into a test record was removed, and a new test fails if a home directory, an e-mail address, a private network address or a time zone ever gets into the repository.

Everything in 0.3.20 is included.

**Tested on a real Mac (M4, macOS 27):** a 1280x720 test program (left half red, right half blue) on a 1920x1050 screen, with every filter, reading back what reaches the window: the picture fills the screen with its proportions kept, red on the left, blue on the right, black bars at the sides; Windows tests 21/21, Vulkan tests 6/6, runtime tests 104/104; NTSYNC with Proton Experimental (Windows events, wait-for-all, abandoned mutex, shared semaphore) and an event round trip of 21 µs (fsync 14 µs, wineserver 50 µs); the Steam Frame root seeing 239 GB with 221 GB free. Records: `benchmarks/stage44-scaling-frame-disk.txt`, `benchmarks/stage45-ntsync.txt`.

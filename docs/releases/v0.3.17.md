**Android apps get audio timestamps.** Android's audio system never knew when the sound it played actually came out: the audio driver of the Android image did not report it, so every app that keeps picture and sound together through Android's audio clock got nothing back. SteamARM now adds that report to the driver for Android's audio service. Measured: Android's audio service went from 440 errors and no timestamps to timestamps with none. (VLC still pauses itself; this was not its cause.)

**Steam ARM64 started outside the launcher is recognised.** When the native ARM64 Steam was started from the command line (`scripts/run-steam-arm64.sh`), the launcher did not recognise it as running. It now does, for both ARM64 roots, and shows it as ARM64.

Everything in 0.3.16 is included.

**Tested on a real Mac (M4, macOS 27):** Android 89 passed with 2 expected failures (the new audio check included); launcher tests pass. Record: `benchmarks/stage40-android-audio-timestamps.txt`.

**Pre-release.** Games are still not verified. Google Play Store has not been run.

Update: drag the new SteamARM.app over the old one, open it, and click **Instalar** in the banner.

**Fix: Counter-Strike 2's own FSR drawn by MetalFX now shows the whole picture.**

What was done:
- **FSR by MetalFX fixed.** In 0.3.34, once Counter-Strike 2's FidelityFX Super Resolution was turned on, the picture showed small in a corner with garbage around it. The game puts its reduced picture in one corner of a full-size texture. SteamARM now reads from the game's FSR settings how big that picture is, and MetalFX enlarges exactly that part to the whole display. Measured in Counter-Strike 2: 1132x637 enlarged to 1920x1080, with the HUD sharp and about 110 frames a second on an M4. If those settings cannot be read, the game's own FSR runs as before.
- **How to use it:** in Counter-Strike 2, open Video and pick a FidelityFX Super Resolution level. In SteamARM, keep **Filtro de escalado** on MetalFX or Automático. Choose FSR there instead to keep the game's own FSR.

Everything in 0.3.34 is included: real fullscreen without the Dock and the menu bar, MetalFX Temporal, MetalFX quality from 33 % to 100 %, and game windows you can resize.

**Tested on a real Mac (M4, macOS 27):** Counter-Strike 2 with FSR on, Vulkan suite 32/32 (the FSR test now has the picture in a corner of its input, as the game does), settings tests passed.

Update: drag the new SteamARM.app over the old one, open it, and click **Instalar** in the banner.

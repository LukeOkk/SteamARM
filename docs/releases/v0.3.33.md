**MetalFX Temporal, MetalFX quality from 33 % to 100 %, real fullscreen without the Dock, and game windows you can resize.**

What was done:
- **MetalFX Temporal.** New choice in Ajustes → Filtro de escalado: **Apple MetalFX (temporal)**, next to the spatial one. It steadies edges and fine detail where the picture does not move, and relies on the current frame where something moves, so there are no trails. Counter-Strike 2 does not hand over its depth, motion or jitter, so it cannot add the extra detail of an upscaler built into a game.
- **Calidad de MetalFX, from 33 % to 100 %.** The old "Escala de render" now goes from **Rendimiento máximo (33 %, 3x)** through Rendimiento (50 %), Equilibrado (59 %), Calidad (67 %) and Calidad ultra (77 %) to **Nativa (100 %)**. MetalFX on Apple silicon enlarges up to 3x. At 100 % with MetalFX Temporal, the picture still goes through MetalFX at its own size, as antialiasing at native resolution.
- **Fullscreen without the Dock.** When a game covers the whole screen (Counter-Strike 2's "Ventana a pantalla completa"), the Dock and the menu bar are now hidden while you play, like any fullscreen Mac game. They come back when you switch to another app.
- **Game windows can be resized like Mac windows.** A game in a window (which fixes its own size) now has resize edges and the green zoom button. SteamARM scales its picture to the new size.

Everything in 0.3.32 is included.

**Tested on a real Mac (M4, macOS 27):** MetalFX temporal pixel-correct on KosmicKrisp and MoltenVK (1.5 ms per 1080p frame), settings tests 15/15, launcher tests 36/36.

**Changing these settings:** close and reopen Steam from SteamARM so that games get them.

Update: drag the new SteamARM.app over the old one, open it, and click **Instalar** in the banner.

**Steam ARM64 · Steam Frame is the primary Steam.** **Configuración → Interfaz** has a new **Steam principal** choice. By default it is Steam ARM64 on the Steam Frame base, then Steam ARM64 on the Fedora base, then the x86_64 client. **Iniciar Steam al abrir el launcher** opens that one; when it cannot start on this Mac, the next one in that order opens instead, and Settings tells you which. The library lists the three in the same order, after your favourites.

**Steam ARM64 on Steam Frame has a GPU.** That base had only a phone graphics driver, so the client found no graphics device. SteamARM's Vulkan bridge is now installed into it as a driver: the client's system information reports the Apple M4. Setup keeps it up to date. Measured: the sign-in window came up 10 times in 12 with it (and 4 in 4 without).

Everything in 0.3.17 is included.

**Tested on a real Mac (M4, macOS 27):** launcher tests and the launcher's script tests pass; the Steam Frame base's own Vulkan tool lists the Apple M4. Record: `benchmarks/stage41-frame-root-vulkan.txt`.

**Pre-release.** Games are still not verified. Signing in to Steam ARM64 has not been tried.

Update: drag the new SteamARM.app over the old one, open it, and click **Instalar** in the banner.

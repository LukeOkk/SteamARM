**Counter-Strike 2 with 4x MSAA runs about 9 % faster on KosmicKrisp.**

What was done:
- **MSAA on Apple GPUs, explained and improved.** Apple's GPUs keep each part of the screen in fast on-chip memory while drawing, and write it out only when a rendering pass ends. Counter-Strike 2 switches render targets about 90 times a frame, and with 4x MSAA each switch writes out and reads back every sample of the colour and depth buffers -- around 2 GB a frame before compression. That is why MSAA costs so much more here than on a PC graphics card. KosmicKrisp was also marking those buffers in a way that stopped Metal from compressing them; it no longer does. Measured with 4x MSAA: 44 → 48 fps in the game's menu (three runs each), 49 fps on Dust2.
- For the best balance on this Mac, CS2's own FSR or CMAA instead of MSAA still gives more frames (around 80 fps with CMAA and FSR "Calidad ultra").

Everything in 0.3.48 is included.

**Tested on a real Mac (M4, macOS 27):** Vulkan tests 40/40; Counter-Strike 2 with 4x MSAA in the menu and on Dust2.

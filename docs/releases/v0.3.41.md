**Fullscreen games are drawn again: no more grey or black screen at start or after a click, and the Dock and the menu bar stay off the game.**

What was done:
- **The game's picture no longer disappears.** Counter-Strike 2 in **Ventana a pantalla completa** could show an empty grey or black screen at start until you switched apps, and again after any click. The game was still drawing; macOS stopped showing it. Two things caused it: hiding the Dock and the menu bar, and a window that covered the display exactly. Both are gone.
- **The Dock and the menu bar stay off the game another way.** The game now goes over them instead of hiding them, while SteamARM is the active app and the game is on top. Clicks anywhere in the game, also at the very bottom, go to the game and never to the Dock under it. Switch apps (Cmd-Tab) and they are back as usual.
- **No more jumping picture.** With the Dock set to show at the screen's edge, the picture could jump back and forth. That mode is no longer used.
- MetalFX now records in the logs when one of its passes fails or takes too long on the GPU.

If a game's text looks blocky with **Calidad de MetalFX** at 100 %, check its own resolution: Counter-Strike 2 keeps its last choice (for example 1280x720) and stretches it itself. Set it to your display's resolution in the game.

Everything in 0.3.40 is included.

**Tested on a real Mac (M4, macOS 27), with the user watching the screen:** Counter-Strike 2 in Ventana a pantalla completa from start, after clicks (also at the bottom and the top of the screen) and after app switches: picture fluid, no Dock, no menu bar, no line at the edge.

Update: drag the new SteamARM.app over the old one, open it, and click **Instalar** in the banner.

**Steam's voice and audio settings now reach the Mac's speakers and microphone.**

What was done:
- **Steam audio through PipeWire works.** The Steam client listed "Mac (sonido)" and "Mac (micrófono)", but neither was picked as the default and nothing was connected to them, so voice chat and Steam's own sounds through PipeWire stayed silent. There were two causes. First, PipeWire wrongly took every program, including its own session manager, for a sandboxed Flatpak app, and so refused its requests. Second, PipeWire had no clock to run its audio graph. Both are fixed. Now the two Mac devices are the defaults and a sound played through them reaches the Mac.
- **Your microphone stays closed** until something in Steam actually records.

Everything in 0.3.35 is included: Counter-Strike 2's own FSR drawn by MetalFX, real fullscreen without the Dock and the menu bar, MetalFX Temporal, MetalFX quality from 33 % to 100 %, and game windows you can resize.

**Tested on a real Mac (M4, macOS 27):** five cold starts out of five with both Mac devices as defaults (none out of five before), a 10-second test stream played in real time and arrived at the Mac's sound server, runtime tests 108 of 109 (the one failure passed on the next run).

Update: drag the new SteamARM.app over the old one, open it, and click **Instalar** in the banner.

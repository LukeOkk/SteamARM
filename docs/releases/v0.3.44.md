**Counter-Strike 2's FSR "Calidad ultra" now also goes through MetalFX.**

What was done:
- **MetalFX at every FSR level.** SteamARM already drew Counter-Strike 2's FSR upscaling with Apple's MetalFX in Calidad, Equilibrado and Rendimiento. Calidad ultra was left out: in that mode the game enlarges the picture with a plain bilinear stretch instead of FSR's own pass, so the driver did not recognise it. The driver now spots that stretch too and replaces it with MetalFX: the game draws at 77 % (1478x831 on a 1080p display) and MetalFX enlarges it.
- **Why changing the resolution in Counter-Strike 2 seemed to do nothing:** in "Ventana a pantalla completa" the game always uses the display's resolution and sets it back when it starts (measured: 1280x720 went back to 1920x1080). To draw at less than the display in that mode, use the game's FSR (now always with MetalFX) or SteamARM's render scale.

Everything in 0.3.43 is included.

**Tested on a real Mac (M4, macOS 27):** Counter-Strike 2 at Calidad ultra on Dust2, "bilinear upsample by MetalFX: picture 1478x831 -> 1920x1080", picture correct; Vulkan tests 40/40 (FSR and cut-out foliage included).

Update: drag the new SteamARM.app over the old one, open it, and click **Instalar** in the banner.

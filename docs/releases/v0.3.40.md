**X11 windows tile like Mac windows: drag them to an edge or a corner, or use Fill and Center in the Window menu.**

What was done:
- **Drag to an edge or a corner.** Dragging an X11 window by its title bar to the left or right edge of the screen shows a blue outline and, on release, gives it that half. Near the top or bottom of a side edge it takes a quarter, and the top edge fills the screen. Dragging it away gives back its previous size. Steam's and Chromium's windows without a Mac title bar work too.
- **Window menu.** **Rellenar**, **Centrar** and **Trasladar y redimensionar** (halves, quarters and **Volver al tamaño anterior**), with the same names as the Mac's own. A double click on a title bar fills the window if the Mac is set to do that.
- **The Mac's tiling margins are respected.** With "Tiled windows have margins" off, tiles touch each other and the screen edges.
- QUARTZ_WM_TILING=0 turns off dragging to edges; XQUARTZ_NO_TILING_MENU=1 leaves the Window menu as it was.

No keyboard shortcuts: macOS keeps Globe-Control-F, C and R for its own menu items. Without Globe they would be Control-F, C and R, which belong to the programs (Control-C in a terminal, crouch and reload in a game).

About Counter-Strike 2: its **Pantalla completa** option is not missing because of SteamARM. Valve removed it from the Linux version in update 1.41 (September 17). **Ventana a pantalla completa** fills the whole display and hides the Dock and the menu bar.

Everything in 0.3.39 is included.

**Tested on a real Mac (M4, macOS 27):** drags to the left edge, the top edge and a corner, and back out, on a window with a Mac title bar and on one without. Every Window menu command on a test window. Control-C still reaches the X11 program. A review of the change found 9 defects; all are fixed in this release.

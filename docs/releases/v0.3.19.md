**Edge smoothing (MSAA) works.** **Configuración → Gráficos → Suavizado de bordes (MSAA)** is no longer greyed out: the game's own choice, 2x, 4x or 8x, forced by DXVK in Direct3D 9 games. Direct3D 10, 11 and 12 games set it in their own options (there is no global switch for them).

**The Steam entries have their own settings.** Each built-in Steam (Steam ARM64 · Steam Frame, Steam ARM64, Steam x86_64) now has **Ajustes de esta app** in its **Ajustes…** sheet: display, V-Sync, synchronization and graphics, over the global ones. They also reach the games that Steam starts.

**Resolution scale / FSR stays unavailable, with the real reason:** none of the installed Proton versions has its full-screen upscaler any more and gamescope does not exist on macOS; a lower in-game resolution is the way for now. MSync stays unavailable (it is a macOS Wine feature that Linux Proton does not have).

Everything in 0.3.18 is included.

**Tested on a real Mac (M4, macOS 27):** the Direct3D 9 test program runs with 4x and 8x MSAA; launcher tests and the launcher's script tests pass (52/52). Record: `benchmarks/stage42-settings-scale-msaa.txt`.

**Pre-release.** Games are still not verified. Signing in to Steam ARM64 has not been tried.

Update: drag the new SteamARM.app over the old one, open it, and click **Instalar** in the banner.

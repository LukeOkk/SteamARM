**More frames by default: "Automático" now picks the fastest setting measured, and the palms on Dust2 are fixed with MSAA.**

What was done:
- **"Automático" scaling is now FSR 1.0 at Equilibrado (59 %).** Every filter and scale was measured in Counter-Strike 2 (Dust2 with bots, maximum settings with MSAA 4x, M4). FSR 1.0 was the fastest filter at every scale, 7-9 frames a second ahead of MetalFX spatial. Below 59 % the frames barely go up: the processor becomes the limit and the picture just gets blurrier. On an M4 at 1080p, Automático now renders at 1152x648 and enlarges with FSR: about 69 fps on average instead of 50. Other Macs get the scale their GPU allows on the same rule.
- **Palm leaves render correctly with MSAA.** At MSAA 2x/4x/8x, the driver drew some foliage passes with one alpha-to-coverage method and others with another. The two did not cover the same samples, so leaf edges were eaten away or speckled. All of them now use the same method.
- **The FSR sharpness slider** also shows with Automático, and the notes explain the choice. MetalFX temporal says what it costs: it was the slowest and the only one with stutters in the tests.

Measured (90 s each, average fps, worst tenth in brackets):
- 67 %: FSR 57.4 (44.5), bilinear 57.0 (43.5), MetalFX spatial 49.7 (43), MetalFX temporal 50.3 (43)
- 59 %: FSR 68.8 (59.5), MetalFX spatial 59.4 (50)
- 50 %: FSR 69.0 (52), MetalFX spatial 61-68
- 33 %: FSR 76.2 (68), very blurry
- MetalFX temporal at 100 %: 31.2 (24), with drops to 2 fps.
- MoltenVK: Counter-Strike 2 crashes loading a match, so KosmicKrisp stays.

If you chose a filter or a scale yourself, it stays as you set it; pick **Automático** to get the new default.

Everything in 0.3.41 is included.

**Tested on a real Mac (M4, macOS 27):** the table above; Vulkan tests 40/40 with the new driver (cut-out foliage at 4x MSAA included); settings tests 15/15; launcher tests 36/36.

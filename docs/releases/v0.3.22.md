**Steam ARM64 and Steam Frame draw their window with the GPU.** Until now the Steam client's own window (Chromium) lost its GPU process at every start on macOS and painted everything with the CPU, which is why it felt slow. **Configuración → Gráficos → Interfaz de Steam ARM64 acelerada por GPU** (on by default) runs it on Vulkan over Metal through the driver you choose above: MoltenVK or **KosmicKrisp**. It applies when Steam opens; if its window ever looks wrong, turn it off.

**KosmicKrisp: games no longer stutter at a lower resolution.** With KosmicKrisp a game drawn smaller than the screen ("Escala de resolución") made DXVK rebuild its swapchain over and over (315 times in 2.5 seconds in the test). SteamARM now enlarges it itself on KosmicKrisp too: bilinear, FSR or whole pixels (MetalFX remains MoltenVK's).

**Games started by Steam Frame get your Processor settings.** The FEX options in Configuración → Procesador now reach the games the Steam ARM64 and Steam Frame clients start, and the FEX server those games need starts with the client.

**Cleaner tests.** The Windows test suite no longer leaves Wine services running after it ends.

Everything in 0.3.21 is included.

**Tested on a real Mac (M4, macOS 27):** KosmicKrisp passes the Vulkan device tests (6/6) and runs the Direct3D 9, 11 and 12 test programs at 150-158 fps; the Steam client's GPU process stays up with the new switches. Records: `benchmarks/stage46-steam-ui-gpu.txt`, `benchmarks/stage47-kosmickrisp.txt`.

**Pre-release.** Games are still not verified by SteamARM's tests; 32-bit Direct3D 12 does not work on KosmicKrisp yet.

Update: drag the new SteamARM.app over the old one, open it, and click **Instalar** in the banner.

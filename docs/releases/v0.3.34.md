**Counter-Strike 2 really fullscreen (no Dock, no menu bar), and a game's own FSR drawn by MetalFX.**

What was done:
- **"Ventana a pantalla completa" now covers the whole display.** Counter-Strike 2 was left 22 pixels short (the height of a title bar), so it was never treated as fullscreen and the Dock stayed over it. The window manager now gives a fullscreen game the whole display, and SteamARM hides the Dock and the menu bar while you play. They come back as soon as you switch to another app.
- **A game's FSR 1 drawn by MetalFX.** When a game upscales with its own FidelityFX Super Resolution 1, SteamARM's graphics driver now replaces that upscale with Apple MetalFX. The game's sharpening and its HUD, drawn afterwards at full resolution, are left as they are. This is on when **Filtro de escalado** is MetalFX or Automático, and off if you choose FSR. In our sessions Counter-Strike 2 had not yet run its FSR pass, so it has not been seen inside the game yet; a test program with an FSR-like shader confirms the replacement.

Everything in 0.3.33 is included: MetalFX Temporal, MetalFX quality from 33 % to 100 %, and game windows you can resize.

**Tested on a real Mac (M4, macOS 27):** Counter-Strike 2 at 1920x1080 with the Dock and the menu bar hidden, Vulkan suite 32/32 (including the new FSR test), settings tests passed.

**Changing these settings:** close and reopen Steam from SteamARM so that games get them.

Update: drag the new SteamARM.app over the old one, open it, and click **Instalar** in the banner.

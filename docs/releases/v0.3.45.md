**Mission Control shows Steam and games properly.**

What was done:
- **No more white window in Mission Control.** With Steam (or a game) open, Mission Control showed the window's picture as a thumbnail, but left a white rectangle where the window had been, and sometimes an empty thumbnail next to it. The white rectangle was the X11 window's own frame, which macOS does not move in Mission Control and which is blank under the picture; the empty thumbnail was a second, fully covered layer of Steam's web view. While Mission Control is open, SteamARM's X server now makes those frames transparent and hides the covered layers; everything comes back as soon as it closes.

Everything in 0.3.44 is included.

**Tested on a real Mac (M4, macOS 27):** Mission Control opened and closed several times with Steam open: a single Steam thumbnail with its picture, no white rectangle or empty thumbnail, and Steam visible and responsive afterwards.

Update: drag the new SteamARM.app over the old one, open it, and click **Instalar** in the banner.

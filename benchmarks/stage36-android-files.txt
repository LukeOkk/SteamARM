== Deleting a directory
Organic Maps (F-Droid 26082718) logged "External storage checks failed"
and "Failed to delete the test dir: /data/data/app.organicmaps/files/
om_test_dir" for internal storage too. From a shell, as the app's uid,
mkdir/rmdir worked; Java's File.delete() goes through remove(), which
tries unlink() and falls back to rmdir() only on EISDIR -- Darwin answers
unlink of a directory with EPERM. The runtime now gives EISDIR there
(tests/elf/unlink_dir.c: 5 of 5; before, unlink and unlinkat EPERM and
remove() failing). After: "Accepted /data/data/app.organicmaps/files/ -
currently configured, internal", the app picks its storage.

== The zygote's descriptor check, once more
2 of 39 saved boot logs had "(zygote) Unsupported st_mode for FD 39/40:
DIR" and the framework restarting (one of them looked like a pm failure
from the launch's side). Left in the runtime: a guest thread that exits
removed its /proc/self/task entry with nftw(), which opens each directory
at the lowest free descriptor while the other threads run -- and the
zygote stops its daemon threads right before it checks every descriptor
for a fork. Removal now uses the runtime's private descriptors (high,
hidden, under the listing's lock), as do the signal-mailbox and SysV
sweeps. tests/elf/fd_scan_threads.c (3 s of /proc/self/fd scans while
threads come and go, no directory but the listing's own) passes on both
runtimes: the window is a few microseconds and was not reproduced in
isolation; the record will tell over the next boots.

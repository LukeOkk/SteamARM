Stage 28 -- ART with its heap above 4 GiB: site counts and patch checks (2026-09-30)

Research only. No Android program, no ART, no lxrun guest was run for this
record; nothing here was compiled for Android. Study:
docs/ART_HEAP_ABOVE_4GB.md. Patches: patches/art-heap-base/.

Sources (shallow clones, read 2026-09-30)

                                                             18.1 image, as in stage 25)
  AOSP platform/art       android11-release  34f62af4d642  (Snap 6683823, rvc-release)

What was run, and where
  1. patches/art-heap-base/count_sites.py on both trees (unpatched). The two
     tables below are its output; the listing after the first is every hit,
     file:line, for the LineageOS tree. The patterns and what each action
     means are in the script's docstring.
  2. The patch series, exported with git format-patch from a branch of the
     LineageOS clone, then re-applied with `git am` to a fresh 7f2b489
     (applies; the result is identical to the branch it was exported from)
     and with `git am -3` to AOSP 34f62af (applies, 9 of 9).
  3. On this Mac (Apple clang, macOS 27.0): the assembly macros of patch 0007
     (DECOMPRESS_HEAP_REF*, the invoke stubs' 24-byte LOADREF entries, the
     introspection entrypoint's `orr xIP1, xIP0, #base`) assembled for
     aarch64-linux-gnu with ART_HEAP_REFERENCE_BASE=0x4000000000: every ORR
     encodes as one instruction (b25a0042 orr x2, x2, #0x4000000000), a
     LOADREF entry is 24 bytes.
  4. libartbase/base/globals.h of patch 0001, -fsyntax-only with a test file
     of constexpr checks: passes with base 0x4000000000 and with no base;
     base 0x180000000 (not a single bit) fails its static_assert, as
     intended.
  Nothing else: the series was not built in an AOSP tree (none here).

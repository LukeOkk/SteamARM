Stage 47 -- KosmicKrisp (Mesa 26.2.3, Vulkan 1.4 on Metal 4) through the
shim: what works, what did not, and the fixes
Date: 2026-10-01. Apple M4, macOS 27, native XQuartz. Zero VM. No game.

    vk_device x86_64 / i386   readback ok ("device Apple M4, driver
                              KosmicKrisp, api 1.4", Mesa 26.2.3)
    vk_reload x86_64 / i386   ok
    gl_zink  x86_64 / i386    OpenGL 4.5, readback ok
    6 passed, 0 failed

2. WINDOWS PROBES (tests/win/run.sh, STEAMARM_VK_ICD=kosmickrisp)
    d3d11 150.6 fps, d3d12 153.3, d3d9 157.8, d3d11_32 154.0,
    d3d9_32 156.3 (MoltenVK: 158-160 on the same probes)
    d3d12_32  FAIL: "Unrecognized VkStructureType 1000351002 referenced by
              pNext, left out" (VkMutableDescriptorTypeCreateInfoEXT, which
              FEX's 32-bit Vulkan thunks do not carry), then a SIGSEGV at
              address 0 in the driver. Open; 32-bit Direct3D 12 only.
    modeset*  FAIL: the scaler and the present scaling were MoltenVK's
              only; with a 1280x720 swapchain on a 1920x1050 layer
              KosmicKrisp made DXVK recreate the swapchain 315 times in
              2.5 s.
  Fix (shim/present.c, shim/scaler.c): any driver but MoltenVK gets the
  scaler's own pass -- a bilinear blit for "Bilineal", and FSR or whole
  pixels as chosen; MetalFX, which needs MoltenVK's Metal objects, falls
  back to the bilinear blit. Not yet run on the native screen with
  KosmicKrisp (the Mac was in use).

3. THE STEAM CLIENT'S OWN WINDOW
  Settings "Interfaz de Steam ARM64 acelerada por GPU" (stage 46) is on by
  default from 0.3.22; its Vulkan goes to the driver chosen in Settings
  (STEAMARM_VK_ICD), KosmicKrisp included.

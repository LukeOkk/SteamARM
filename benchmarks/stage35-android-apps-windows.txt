== Playback
An H.264 + AAC MP4 (20 s, 320x240, made with AVFoundation) in /sdcard/Movies:
- Android's MediaPlayer (app_process64): "position 944 ... 5961" over 6 s
  of wall time, playing true; pactl list sink-inputs during it:
  media.name = "ALSA Playback".
- AudioTrack (MODE_STREAM, 44.1 kHz mono), blocking and WRITE_NON_BLOCKING:
  the playback head advances ~44100 frames per second in both.
- VLC (x86_64 libvlc under FEX): demuxes, decodes ("Received first
  picture"), opens AudioTrack and its EGL video output, then its player
  stays paused (AudioTrack started, paused 48 ms later; media session
  state 2, position 0; a media key toggles it and it pauses again). NOT
  EXPLAINED: Android's own media path plays the same file in real time.
  LATER (2026-10-01, a per-app window session): reproduced, state 2 and
  position 0 after 12 s. Not audio focus: VLC holds it, "loss: none".
  AudioFlinger's output thread has "Timestamp stats: n=0 ... err=440":
  Waydroid's audio HAL (audio.primary.waydroid, alsa-lib to PulseAudio)
  never reports a presentation position (it calls neither
  snd_pcm_htimestamp nor snd_pcm_delay), so AudioTrack.getTimestamp()
  has nothing and the track reports "Latency unavail". VLC's track was
  consumed for 929 frames (19 ms), underran ("disabled due to previous
  underrun, restarting"), and VLC paused it 1.8 s after starting it;
  its video output reports pictures 267-650 ms late. VLC 3.6 no longer
  offers OpenSL ES (its "aout" preference is ignored). Still NOT
  EXPLAINED; the missing HAL timestamps are the lead (MediaPlayer copes
  without them).

== Traceur
com.android.traceur crashed at every boot: its receiver runs `perfetto
--is_detached`, which failed without perfetto's service ("Perfetto error"
248 while apps could not exec at all, stage 34; 1 after). traced is now
started after the boot ("Started traced, listening on
/dev/socket/traced_producer /dev/socket/traced_consumer"); boots since
had no crash. (tombstoned was tried too and left out: its listening
SEQPACKET sockets are datagram sockets here, runtime/socket.c, and it
aborts with "failed to create evconnlistener".)

== No Mac window, one launch in five
Solitaire launched over and over in fresh sessions: the app had the focus
and the compositor log no window -- 5 of 28 launches. `dumpsys
SurfaceFlinger` timed out (10 s) in such a session; the binder hub's new
state dump (SIGUSR1, <dir>/hub-state.txt) showed SurfaceFlinger's main
thread inside a call to the composer (hwbinder, code 0x17), handled by the
composer's main thread, which had not replied. steamarm-wlmac --verbose
(WLMAC_EXTRA_ARGS, scripts/run-wlmac.sh): the composer had made the app's
toplevel, committed, sent wl_display.sync, acked both configures -- and
sent nothing after (a good launch goes on with wl_shm_pool.create_buffer).
The socket was empty both ways. lldb on the composer: two threads in
poll(), one in __ulock_wait on a word holding exactly its expected value
(0xfffffffe: a bionic semaphore with a waiter -- the EGL worker idling on
egl_go, not the hang).
Waydroid's composer (hwcomposer/wayland-hwc.cpp, create_window) waits for
the first configure with `do wl_display_roundtrip(); while (!configured)`
on the default queue, while its hwc_wayland_thread runs
wl_display_dispatch() on that same queue. When the wayland thread
dispatches the sync's done first, the roundtrip's thread stays in poll()
with nothing more to come; SurfaceFlinger's call never returns, and with
SurfaceFlinger the window manager's lock and system_server's watchdog
(stage 34's "Not explained" hang after Termux's window appeared).
steamarm-wlmac now follows every wl_display.sync with xdg_wm_base pings at
50, 250 and 1000 ms (the client answers pong): any event wakes the poll and
the roundtrip finds its flag set. After: 12 launches of 12 with a window.

2. VULKAN THROUGH THE ROOT'S LOADER WAS BROKEN
  tests/elf/vk_device.c built for aarch64 against the root's Khronos
  loader, the shim installed as its ICD:
    "[Vulkan Loader] ERROR: loader_add_instance_extensions:
     /usr/lib/libvulkan_steamarm.so's vkEnumerateInstanceExtensionProperties
     points to the loader, this would lead to infinite recursion."
    then no progress (killed after 60 s)
  The shim exports the same names as the loader, and its own references to
  them bound to the loader's (ELF interposition). The web helper's ANGLE
  Vulkan in stage 46 most probably went to the SwiftShader that CEF ships
  for that reason. Fix: the shim is linked -Bsymbolic (Makefile).
    MoltenVK      "device Apple M4, driver MoltenVK" ... "== vk_device: ok"
    KosmicKrisp   "device Apple M4, driver KosmicKrisp, api 1.4" ... ok
  ELF suite 104/104, Vulkan device 6/6 on each driver, Windows suite 21/21
  (MoltenVK) after the change.

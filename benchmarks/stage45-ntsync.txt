Stage 45 -- NTSYNC: /dev/ntsync emulated across processes
Date: 2026-10-01. Apple M4, macOS 27. Zero VM. No game.

WHAT
  Linux 6.14's NT synchronization driver (drivers/misc/ntsync.c): Wine's
  server makes every Windows semaphore, mutex and event an object of it and
  hands the fds to its processes, which wait and signal without a round
  trip to the server. Proton Experimental and Hotfix open /dev/ntsync when
  it exists ("ntsync: up and running"; Proton 10.0 does not have it).
  runtime/ntsync.c offers it only with LXRT_NTSYNC=1 (Settings ->
  Sincronización -> NTSYNC, experimental): without it the open fails with
  ENOENT as before.

DESIGN
  A device is a directory of the user's: an arena file (header with the
  device lock, 65536 object slots, 1024 waiter records) mapped MAP_SHARED
  by every process that has the device or one of its objects, and one small
  file per object, whose fd is the object's fd. Each fd holds a shared
  flock on its file; flock belongs to the open file description, which dup,
  fork and SCM_RIGHTS share, so a file is free exactly when no process has
  an fd of it -- the kernel's last fput, without tracking closes. One
  futex-style lock per device (shared Darwin ulocks); signals hand objects
  to waiters in queue order before the ioctl returns, as the driver's
  try_wake_any_* / try_wake_all. A lock held by a dead process is taken over
  after 200 ms; a dead process's waiters are dropped.

CORRECTNESS
  The kernel's own selftests (tools/testing/selftests/drivers/ntsync,
  current master, built for aarch64 with the 6.14 harness: the newer one
  needs pidfd_open), under lxrun with LXRT_NTSYNC=1, 3 runs:
    pass 12, fail 0, skip 1 (wait_any_monotonic_timens: time namespaces)
  tests/elf/ntsync.c (in tests/elf/run.sh): 17 of 17, aarch64 and x86_64
  under FEX -- states, overflow, recursion, wrong owner, abandoned mutex,
  wait-all taking all or nothing, alerts, and the Wine pattern: device and
  objects handed to another process over SCM_RIGHTS, a semaphore released to
  its waiter there before the release returns.
  Proton Experimental, tests/win/sync.c (events between two threads,
  WaitForMultipleObjects of all, abandoned mutex, a semaphore of 2 shared by
  4 threads), tests/win/run.sh sync*:
    sync          wineserver   ok
    sync_fsync    fsync        ok
    sync_ntsync   ntsync       ok ("ntsync: up and running" in the log)

SPEED (event ping-pong between two threads, 20000 round trips)
    wineserver           50.5 us
    fsync                14.4-15.5 us
    ntsync, first cut   116.7 us  (every ioctl opened and read the files)
    ntsync, cached       20.9 us  (fds identified once per process, by
                                   device, inode and birth time)

NOT DONE
  Not measured with a game. The launcher keeps it experimental and out of
  AUTO.

Result (MEASURED)
-----------------
Heroic 2.22.3, linux-arm64 (Electron 43.1.1), runs natively from the Fedora
armroot: its main window "Heroic Games Launcher" (1200x690) is mapped and
viewable on :2 4.0-4.8 s after start; a click on its sidebar's Settings link
shows the settings route (#/settings/general, tabs General, Game Defaults,
Advanced, System Information, Log); SIGTERM to its main process ends every
process of the session in 0.4-0.5 s with exit status 0, and so does Heroic's
own Quit (sidebar -> "Quit" dialog -> QUIT); it starts again the same way.
7 of 7 cycles passed in the final runs (P1-P3). About 1.7 GB resident over
6 processes. Four of its five helpers run (legendary, gogdl, comet,
vulkan-helper); nile (Amazon) cannot: a non-PIE binary.

It needed six runtime fixes (R1-R6), GTK 3, libsecret, libnotify and python3
in the armroot's seeds (S1), and two Chromium/V8 switches:
--disable-gpu (G1) and --js-flags=--no-opt, because V8's TurboFan tier
crashes under lxrun (T1-T4; cause UNKNOWN).

         prlimit64(0, RLIMIT_NPROC, NULL, <that page>) expecting EFAULT

         guest buffer (EFAULT from the kernel). After: "syscall 261 -> -14".

         that is not a multiple of 4 KiB covers the page its last byte is in
         (16 KiB for a native guest told 16 KiB pages, 4 KiB otherwise); a
         multiple of 4 KiB is left as it was (the sub-page extension).
         A first version rounded every length to 16 KiB: tests/elf/run.sh

         68 passed, 0 failed.

         recvmsg(fd 3) forever. SOCK_SEQPACKET is a Darwin DGRAM pair here,

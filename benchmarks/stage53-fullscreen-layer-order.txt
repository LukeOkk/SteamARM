Stage 53 -- Counter-Strike 2 played by a person: the game's picture behind
its own window, a real fullscreen, and presents in step with the display
Date: 2026-10-02. Apple M4, macOS 27, display 1920x1080 at 165 Hz (fixed
rate). Zero VM. Valve's files unchanged. Steam Frame client, the game's
native Linux build through tools/steamarm-fex-linux, KosmicKrisp 26.2.4 with
SteamARM's patches. Stages 51 and 52 measured the game with bots and no hands
on it; this one is what its owner met when playing.

HOW IT WAS MEASURED
  No capture of the screen. The window server's list of windows
  (CGWindowListCopyWindowInfo: ids, levels, bounds, order; no names, no
  pixels) sampled every 2 ms. The shim's LXRT_VK_PROBE (new): the mean colour
  of eight zones of the game's own image, read back from the swapchain. The
  driver's KK_PRESENT_LOG=1 (new, patch 06): for every present, when the GPU
  finished the frame and when the display showed it (CAMetalDrawable
  presentedTime). Two films of the screen made by the user with a telephone.
  Input: XTEST (tests/android/xtest_input.py and the like).

1. "EVERYTHING GOES WHITE" WHEN MOVING OR SHOOTING
  The picture of a Vulkan window is a second window of the X server's (the
  overlay with the CALayerHost) ordered directly above the X window's frame.
  The frame is put in front by the window manager's restack on a click and
  on a drag of its title bar, by Xplugin on its own thread, and the overlay
  was ordered above it again only afterwards (and at 50 and 300 ms). Seen in
  the order of the windows: the frame above the overlay for a sample of 2 to
  5 ms at every step of a drag, and once for good: [frame][overlay] until a
  restack was forced by hand. What the user sees then is the X window (white;
  black in fullscreen). And the layer, hidden, is given no drawable any
  more: vkAcquireNextImageKHR 1005 ms (nextDrawable's timeout), 1.5 frames a
  second, for as long as it lasted.
  Tried first: a window-server ordering group
  (CGSAddWindowToWindowOrderingGroup(frame, overlay, above); returns 0). It
  did not hold the order through the restacks (the overlay was found below
  its frame at 20 consecutive checks 100 ms apart), and a frame whose
  overlay was ordered out left the screen with it (Steam's main window:
  viewable for X, not on screen). Removed.
  Fix (patches/xquartz-remote-order.patch): while the X server is the active
  application and no other X toplevel is stacked over the layer, the overlay
  is one window level above its frame; otherwise at the frame's level as
  before, and every 100 ms an overlay found below its frame is put back.
  After: 40 raises and clicks in 4 s, and 30 s of a match, sampled every
  2 ms: the order never changed; 0 corrections by the 100 ms check.

2. NO 1920x1080, AND NO WAY TO FILL THE SCREEN
  XQuartz's rootless screen leaves out the strip under the menu bar:
  1920x1050 here, so the game's list ended at 1920x1050 and its window had a
  title bar. Now (same patch, and patches/quartz-wm-fullscreen-head.patch):
  the X screen is the display (1920x1080; STEAMARM_X11_FULL_DISPLAY=0 for the
  old one); the window manager still places ordinary windows in NSScreen's
  visible frame (a new 800x600 window: +0+30); its fullscreen is the whole
  head; and while a layer covers a display the server hides the menu bar and
  the Dock (they return with any other application).
  Counter-Strike 2 with fullscreen=1, 1920x1080: window 1920x1080+0+0, the
  overlay over all of the display.

3. "THE SCREEN FLICKERS BLACK" IN FULLSCREEN
  The user's films (24 frames a second) alternate between the game's picture
  and black, frame by frame, with the pointer visible on the black ones.
  Not the game's image: LXRT_VK_PROBE=2 read back all ~2300 frames of a 70 s
  match and found 4 black ones (the first three and one at a load).
  Not the order of the windows (section 1: stable at 2 ms).
  Not a drawable shown before it was drawn: KK_PRESENT_LOG, 200 presents:
  each on the display within -0.7..+1.9 ms of the end of its GPU work.
  What that log did show: the times between shown frames were not whole
  refreshes (29.7-36.3 ms): with the game's IMMEDIATE, the layer covering
  the display is flipped outside the display's refreshes.
  Change (shim/mailbox.c): the driver's own swapchain under the mailbox is
  FIFO, with three images. The game still never waits for the display; a
  frame that finds no driver image is dropped only if the one before was
  shown less than 13 ms ago, and otherwise waits for it (up to 30 ms).
  After: every interval a whole number of refreshes (distance from one:
  median 0.00, 90th percentile 0.01), frames shown 3.4-13.8 ms after the GPU
  finished them, none dropped (506 of 506 in 16 s; with two driver images
  and no wait: 363 shown, 176 dropped), presents 3.5 ms on average. Five
  more minutes of the same match: 8970 shown, 5 dropped.
  WHETHER THE BLACK IS GONE WAS NOT SEEN BY THIS MEASUREMENT: it needs eyes
  on the screen. LXRT_VK_MAILBOX_REAL=immediate gives the old presents.

4. THE SETTINGS ASKED FOR, AND WHAT THEY COST
  1920x1080 fullscreen, everything at its highest (4x MSAA: the GPU has no
  8x; shadows, particles, ambient occlusion and textures at the top, FSR
  off): de_dust2 with nine bots, median 32.5-34.5 frames a second, GPU-bound
  (stage 52's 86-93.5 were at the game's own lower settings with FSR
  Performance). Footprint 8.0-8.7 GB; safeguard.sh logged memory pressure
  level 2 with 22-24 % free.

5. ALSO
  - A vertex stage no longer waits for the render pass before it unless it
    samples a texture, and everything waits for a pass whose shaders write
    memory (patches/kosmickrisp-05-vertex-barrier.patch; main menu 82.5
    against 78.5 frames a second with the first version of it;
    tests/elf/vk_vtxread.c fails on a build without either exception).
  - Where the passes go, main menu, by leaving out the draws of one kind of
    pass (experiment builds): none at all 122.5 frames a second against
    78.5, so the passes alone cap it there; the multisampled ones 86.5.
    624,000 passes: 395,577 with one draw, 94,900 with only a clear, 19,946
    with neither.

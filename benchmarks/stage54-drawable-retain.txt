Stage 54 -- "The window freezes or goes black whenever I change the video
settings, and only comes back when I switch windows": a drawable retained
for good by the driver at every present, and V-Sync through the mailbox
Date: 2026-10-02 (evening). Apple M4, macOS 27, 1920x1080 at 165 Hz. Zero
VM. Valve's files unchanged. Steam Frame client, Counter-Strike 2's native
Linux build, KosmicKrisp 26.2.4 with SteamARM's patches 01-07.

HOW IT WAS MEASURED
  tests/elf/vk_x11_modes.c (new): an X window that goes fullscreen
  (_NET_WM_STATE_FULLSCREEN, as SDL does), back, borderless at the display's
  size, resized, and that makes its swapchain again (oldSwapchain) at every
  change, presenting FIFO frames; a phase whose frames stall fails.
  KK_DRAWABLE_LOG=1 (new, patch 07): every nextDrawable with its wait and
  the drawable's retain count, every release, every configuration of the
  layer. The shim's LXRT_VK_TIMING now also prints how long the mailbox's
  thread waited in the driver's acquire. The X server's XQUARTZ_RL_DEBUG=1.

1. REPRODUCED WITHOUT THE GAME
  Every sequence failed at its THIRD swapchain, even "the same size again,
  a new swapchain only": frames of 1000 ms (nextDrawable's timeout), the
  mailbox's thread waiting 1014 ms for a drawable. With the mailbox off
  (LXRT_VK_MAILBOX=0) and on MoltenVK with it on: every phase at 6 ms.
  So: the driver, with a swapchain that is replaced while it holds an
  acquired image -- what the mailbox always does, one image ahead.

2. CAUSE (KosmicKrisp, Mesa 26.2.4)
  kk_encode_drawable_present retains the drawable for the command buffer
  that presents it; kk_queue presents and sets the field to NULL without a
  release, and a command buffer freed with a present recorded and never
  submitted (the acquired image of a destroyed swapchain) never releases it
  either; nor does wsi_metal_destroy_image give back the retain of the
  acquire. A drawable with a retain outstanding never returns to the
  layer's pool of three. One is lost at every swapchain made with an image
  in hand: after two, one drawable serves a FIFO swapchain that needs two.
  With the user: every change of the video settings (a new swapchain), and
  "switching windows" made SDL remake the swapchain yet again.

3. FIX (patches/kosmickrisp-07-release-acquired-drawable.patch)
  The retain is released after the present (the layer keeps what the
  presentation needs), on a command buffer reset or destroyed with a
  present still recorded, and on a swapchain image destroyed while
  acquired.
  After: vk_x11_modes, eight phases (window, resize, two remakes at the
  same size, fullscreen on, off, borderless, decorated): every phase 5.9-
  6.1 ms a frame, no stall. Added to tests/elf/run_vk_arm64.sh (SteamARM's
  driver and MoltenVK; Homebrew's driver is known to fail it).

4. THE GAME (CORRECTED IN STAGE 55: the 80 below was measured while the
   game's settings were being changed in the game; later runs at the saved
   highest settings measured 28-34. Not a comparison with the 35.)
   SAME SETTINGS AS STAGE 53 (highest, 1920x1080, V-Sync on)
  de_dust2, nine bots, 200 s, last 120 s: median 80.0 fps (average 78.4,
  10th percentile 56.5, min 49, max 122.5) against 35.0 before the fix in
  the same configuration; 9404 frames shown, 0 dropped, the driver's
  acquire 13 ms on average (the display's turn). The leaked drawables had
  cost the game more than half its rate as well as its picture.

5. V-SYNC THROUGH THE MAILBOX (shim/mailbox.c, earlier the same day)
  A FIFO swapchain of the game's takes the mailbox path and never drops a
  frame: a slow game shows every frame on a refresh, a fast one is held to
  the display. The driver's own FIFO made a GPU-bound game wait at every
  acquire until the frame before had been shown: 23.5 fps against 35 then.

Tests: Vulkan 20/20 (vk_x11_modes 2 new), Python 151/151.

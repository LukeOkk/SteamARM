1. "ESCALA DE RESOLUCION" AS 0.3.20 SHIPPED IT WAS BROKEN FOR DXVK
  tests/win/modeset.c now draws its left half red and its right half blue,
  and the shim reads back what a swapchain image holds when it is presented
  (LXRT_VK_SCALER_PROBE=1, shim/scaler.c probe_image):
    0.3.20 (Proton's fullscreen hack on, the default)
      "[shim] vkCreateSwapchainKHR extent 1x1 usage 0x8 flags 0x4"
      Proton's hack (dlls/win32u/vulkan.c, win32u_vkCreateSwapchainKHR)
      makes its swapchain at the surface's minImageExtent, which MoltenVK
      reports as 1x1, and scales the game into it with a compute shader:
      the picture became one pixel.
    min = max = currentExtent for X11 surfaces (shim/wsi.c, as Linux's
    drivers report them): the hack's swapchain became 1867x1050, but the
    probe read 0,0,0,0 everywhere -- its compute blit into the swapchain
    image writes nothing on MoltenVK.
  So SteamARM turns the hack off (WINE_DISABLE_FULLSCREEN_HACK=1 with
  "Escala de resolución", settings-env.py) and enlarges the game itself.
  Without the hack Wine keeps the game's client window at 1280x720 inside a
  toplevel that covers the screen, and detaches it (moves it under a 1x1
  window of its own) to copy its X contents scaled -- contents that are
  empty here, the frames being a Core Animation layer. shim/wsi.c
  (LXRT_VK_PADDED_FULLSCREEN=1) shows the frames on that toplevel instead,
  remembered when the client is detached, while the game still sees its own
  window's size: its swapchain stays 1280x720 and the shim enlarges it to
  the layer.

2. SCALING FILTERS (shim/scaler.c; Settings "Filtro de escalado", per app)
  The game's picture is kept in its aspect and centred, black bars around
  (as Proton's hack drew it). tests/win/run.sh, DXVK on MoltenVK:
    modeset          MoltenVK's stretch (aspect, centred): swapchain 1280x720,
                     surface 1920x1050, left 0,0,255 right 255,0,0
    modeset_fsr      AMD FSR 1.0 (EASU + RCAS, third_party/fsr1):
                     1280x720 -> 1920x1050, left red, right blue, corner
                     black (the bar)
    modeset_nearest  vkCmdBlitImage NEAREST: same
    modeset_metalfx  Apple MetalFX spatial scaler, encoded by the runtime
                     (runtime/metalfx.m) between two of the shim's
                     submissions, ordered by a timeline semaphore's
                     MTLSharedEvent: same. (It first failed with -7:
                     vkGetDeviceProcAddr gives no vkExportMetalObjectsEXT
                     unless the game enabled VK_EXT_metal_objects; the shim
                     calls MoltenVK's exported entry point.)
    376-384 frames in 2.5 s for each; Windows suite 21/21, Vulkan 6/6.
  Also found on the way: MoltenVK answered VK_SUBOPTIMAL_KHR to every
  present of a swapchain smaller than its layer (4 frames in 2.5 s); the
  shim asks for present scaling (shim/present.c).

3. THE STEAM FRAME ROOT SAW 22 GB FREE
  Its root lives in a case-sensitive sparse bundle made with a 40 GB
  capacity; Steam reads free space inside it (statfs):
    before   df in the root: 40G size, 18G used, 22G free (Mac: 219 GB free)
    scripts/image-volume.sh grows a bundle to the size of the volume that
    holds it (sparse: the bundle stayed 20 GB on disk) when nothing has it
    open -- here only after a forced unmount: a system process held the
    volume with no file of the user's open.
    then     APFS answers free space from the Mac's disk but keeps the
             capacity as its size: 1.7T size, "1.5T used"
    runtime/fileops2.c (LXRT_STATFS_BACKING, written by image-volume.sh):
             free capped by the Mac's volume, size = the volume's own used
             space (ATTR_VOL_SPACEUSED) + free: 239G size, 18G used, 221G free
  The Android image (24 GB) was grown the same way, after stopping three
  logd stand-ins left by a test run that morning (tests/android/logd.py now
  exits when its parent does) and SteamARM's PulseAudio, which held a socket
  on it.

  The client checks for SteamOS updates (steamos-update check ->
  atomupd-manager check), atomupd-manager talks to atomupd-daemon on the
  system D-Bus, which does not exist here: SIGSEGV at addr 0x8 each time.
  scripts/install-frameroot-atomupd.sh puts a stand-in in its place (the
  image's kept as atomupd-manager.lxrt-off): "No update available"
  (steamos-update exits 7, its "no update"), "idle" for the status, updates
  and branch switches refused. setup.sh and mkframeroot.sh run it.

SteamARM Stage 1 -- cost of intercepting one Linux syscall on Darwin
pid 51338, timebase 41.6667 ns/tick

[1] Does Darwin distinguish `svc #0` from `svc #0x80`?
    NO. `svc #0` with x16=20 returned our pid (51338).
    Darwin ignores the SVC immediate and dispatches on x16.
    CONSEQUENCE: a Linux binary's `svc #0` does not trap. It
    executes whatever Darwin syscall x16 happens to hold, with
    Linux arguments. Interception by trapping is NOT AVAILABLE;
    the loader must rewrite every svc site, and self-modifying
    or JIT-generated code (FEX) needs a different mechanism.

[2] Does an invalid syscall number raise a catchable signal?
    YES, as SIGTRAP. Handler ran and stepped the pc past the svc.

[3] Mach exception interception (EXC_BREAKPOINT, handler thread)
    Handler thread received the exception and advanced the pc.

[4] Rewritten `svc` site: branch into the runtime dispatcher
    Serviced Linux getpid(172) through the dispatch table.
